The Problem

Running a dealership shouldn't feel this chaotic

If any of these sound familiar, you're not alone. Most pre-owned two wheeler dealerships lose 15-20% of potential revenue to operational gaps.

📋

Stock lives in spreadsheets

You don't know what's in stock, what's in refurb, or what's been sitting unsold for 60 days until you physically check.

💰

No visibility on margins

Purchase price, refurb cost, RTO expenses, selling price. Calculating profit per vehicle is a nightmare across stores.

📝

RC transfers go missing

Documents get lost between agents, RTOs, and stores. Buyers call asking for updates you can't give. Follow ups fall through.

📈

Bookings tracked on WhatsApp

Advance payments, cancellations, and delivery dates exist in chat threads. Nobody has the full picture.

🏪

Multi-store is multi-headache

Each store runs its own way. Comparing performance, transferring vehicles, or getting a unified report takes days.

🔒

Staff does whatever they want

No role based access means anyone can see or change anything. There's no audit trail when things go wrong.

Platform

One system for your entire operation

From the moment you purchase a vehicle to the day you hand over the RC, every step is tracked, documented, and visible.

Inventory that tracks itself

See every vehicle across every store. Know what's ready for sale, what's in refurb, and what's been sitting too long. Bulk upload via Excel, track chassis numbers, manage images and documents.

  • Real time stock status across all locations
  • Holding period alerts to prevent ageing inventory
  • Inter store vehicle and document transfers
  • Image and document management per vehicle

Bookings and sales without the mess

Track every booking from advance to delivery. Generate digital receipts, sales delivery notes, and invoices. Know exactly who booked what, at which store, for how much.

  • Digital booking receipts and delivery notes
  • Advance collection and refund tracking
  • B2B and B2C sales in one place
  • Cancellation and forfeiture management

RC transfers you can actually track

Stop losing documents between agents and RTOs. Track every vehicle's ownership transfer with TAT monitoring, multi-agent support, and complete document trails.

  • Pipeline view from pending to completed
  • Multi RTO agent management
  • Transfer expense tracking
  • Document management per transfer

Know your margins on every vehicle

See the full cost picture: purchase price, refurb costs, RTO expenses, holding costs. Make data driven pricing decisions instead of guessing.

  • Per vehicle gross margin calculation
  • Purchase, refurb, and transfer cost roll-up
  • Dashboard and list views
  • Identify which vehicles make (or lose) money
